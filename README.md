# 🚕 Uber Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Uber Business Intelligence Dashboard** designed to analyze bookings, revenue, vehicle performance, rider behavior, cancellations, payments, and operational trends.

The dashboard provides a centralized view of key business metrics through dedicated **Overview, Vehicle, Revenue, and Rider** analysis pages.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard is organized into four major analytical sections:

* 🏠 **Overview**
* 🚘 **Vehicle Analysis**
* 💰 **Revenue Analysis**
* 👤 **Rider Analysis**

It enables users to identify business trends, understand vehicle performance, analyze revenue sources, and evaluate rider behavior.

---

# 🏠 1. Overview Page

The Overview page provides a high-level snapshot of Uber's business performance.

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators

| KPI                       | Description                                             |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🚕 **Completed Bookings** | Total number of successfully completed rides            |
| ❌ **Lost Bookings**       | Bookings that were cancelled or otherwise not completed |
| 💰 **Revenue**            | Total revenue generated from bookings                   |
| 🛣️ **Total Distance**    | Total distance travelled across completed rides         |
| 📏 **Average Distance**   | Average distance travelled per ride                     |

### 🔎 Filters

* Vehicle Type
* Monthly Analysis
* Quarterly Analysis

## 📅 Monthly Analysis

Track business performance month-by-month using:

* **Completed Bookings**
* **Revenue**

## 📆 Quarterly Analysis

Compare performance across quarters using:

* **Completed Bookings**
* **Revenue**

## 🚘 Revenue by Vehicle Type

Analyze revenue contribution across different vehicle categories to identify the highest-performing vehicle types.

## 📍 Top Pickup & Drop Locations

Identify the most popular:

* Pickup locations
* Drop locations

Locations are ranked based on **booking count**.

## ⭐ Ratings

Monitor customer and driver experience through:

* **Average Rider Rating**
* **Average Driver Rating**

---

# 🚘 2. Vehicle Page

The Vehicle page provides detailed performance metrics for each vehicle type.

## 📈 Vehicle Performance

For every vehicle type, analyze:

| Metric               | Description                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| 🚕 **Booking Count** | Number of bookings associated with the vehicle         |
| 💰 **Revenue**       | Revenue generated by the vehicle                       |
| 📊 **Contribution**  | Vehicle's contribution to overall business performance |

This view helps identify the most profitable and frequently used vehicle categories.

---

# 💰 3. Revenue Page

The Revenue page focuses on understanding where revenue comes from and how it changes over time.

## 👥 Revenue by Customer

Analyze revenue generated by individual customers to identify high-value riders.

## 🚘 Revenue by Vehicle

Compare revenue performance across different vehicle types.

## 💳 Revenue by Payment Method

Understand revenue distribution across available payment methods.

## 📅 Time-Based Revenue Analysis

Analyze revenue trends using:

* **Monthly Revenue**
* **Quarterly Revenue**

This makes it easier to identify growth patterns, seasonal trends, and changes in business performance.

---

# 👤 4. Rider Page

The Rider page focuses on customer behavior, cancellations, payment preferences, and rider segmentation.

## ❌ Ride Cancellation Analysis

Analyze cancelled rides based on cancellation reasons.

This helps identify common issues affecting ride completion and customer experience.

## 💳 Payment Method Analysis

Understand rider payment preferences by analyzing bookings and cancellations across payment methods.

## 📅 Monthly & Quarterly Analysis

Track rider-related metrics across:

* Monthly trends
* Quarterly trends

## 📋 Detailed Rider Data

Provide detailed rider-level information in a structured data table for deeper analysis.

## 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Rider Segmentation

Classify riders into three major categories:

### 🆕 First Rider

Customers completing their first ride.

### 🔄 Return Rider

Customers who have used the service again after their initial ride.

### ⭐ Regular Rider

Customers who frequently use the service and demonstrate consistent booking behavior.
